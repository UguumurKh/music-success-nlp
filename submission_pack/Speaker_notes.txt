4. Preprocessing decisions for the first models
Timing: 1:30. Distinguish completed cleaning from modelling-specific representation. Existing cleaning normalizes Unicode/whitespace and removes verified standalone section labels and music-note-only lines. Raw text remains. Stage 11 applies one supported correction. For the first topic representation we use lowercase unigrams, a fixed stopword list with negation retention, and normalized linear TF-IDF. No lemmatization has been used. We omit length, lexical diversity and repetition as predictors because source transcription problems can distort them. Source: Stage 10–11 audit, Stage 12 report and supplied code.

5. Whole-block repetition: evidence and treatment
Timing: 1:30. A flag means that the entire normalized nonblank line sequence repeats an integer number of times. It does not establish an extraction error. Across the full prepared corpus there are 955 flags: one corrected, 954 unresolved. The English-screened sample contains 903 historical flags: one corrected, 902 unresolved. Fixed-fit normalized linear unigram vectors matched full versus one-unit text to numerical precision for all 955 flags. This supports this representation only, not semantic deletion or reliable song-length measures. Source: Stage 10–11 repetition audit and Stage 12 sample counts.

6. Topic fitting inside each validation fold
Timing: 1:30. We create five grouped folds and reuse them across models. In each fold, vectorizer.fit_transform sees training text only. NMF fits training TF-IDF only. Held-out text uses the saved vectorizer and NMF transform. Imputation, scaling and logistic regression also fit training rows only. The same fold-specific topic representation feeds the lyrics-only and combined classifiers. The all-data descriptive fit occurs after evaluation and never produces validation predictions. Groups link complete artist credits and known shared-text cases, but solo/collaborative performers can still cross folds. Source: supplied Stage 12 modelling script, main() and make_groups().

7. NMF identifies patterns beyond semantic themes
Timing: 1:00. NMF is a nonnegative factorization of the document–term matrix. The separate all-data descriptive fit has ten components. Five are dominated by vocal syllables/interjections. The love and affectionate-address labels are tentative and require reading representative lyrics. Diction is a vocabulary pattern, not proof of a genre or performer identity. NMF loadings are not probabilities. Descriptive component numbering cannot be mapped directly to independently fitted fold components. Source: descriptive_topics.csv and Stage 12 report.

8. Adding lyric components improves preliminary AP
Timing: 1:00. The primary metric is average precision, a summary of precision over recall thresholds. It is not accuracy or trapezoidal PR-AUC. Mean fold AP rises from 0.2334 for audio to 0.2527 for combined input. Topics alone score 0.1891 and the baseline 0.1541. The paired improvement is positive in every fold, averaging 0.0193. This is descriptive consistency, not a statistical significance test. ROC-AUC rises from 0.6281 to 0.6482. Source: independently recomputed from out_of_fold_predictions.csv.

9. Initial results identify further cleaning needs
Timing: 1:30. These are substantive preprocessing findings from the first analysis. Vocal syllables can dominate latent components, so we plan a documented alternative representation for semantic interpretation while retaining the original baseline. We will not automatically delete all interjections because they can contain useful stylistic signal. Independent regex testing demonstrated that the current two-character prefix mishandles contractions beginning with I: I'll becomes ll and I've becomes ve. The report's claim that fragments arise only from separated source text is incomplete. These are baseline results with a known tokenizer limitation. We have not rerun a corrected model or estimated its improvement. Source: descriptive topics, supplied TOKEN_PATTERN and independent Python re tests.

10. Current conclusions and open decisions
Timing: 1:30. We have enough progress for this interim submission. The provisional finding is that lyric-derived components add some information beyond ten audio features in this matched sample. We cannot conclude causality or that thematic meaning specifically drives the gain. Coverage varies by year and outcome. Grouping is incomplete at individual performer level. For the professor's proposed rank gap, excluding 11–15 would remove 330 negatives and leave 6,055 songs, but the cutoff is illustrative and no alternative model has been fitted. Ask whether they want a sensitivity analysis and which gap. Future changes informed by these results should be documented as development, with fresh evaluation design or a final untouched set for stronger final claims. Source: Stage 12 report and saved predictions.
